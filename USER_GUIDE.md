# 📘 Acne Type Classification Application - User & Admin Guide

Welcome to the **Acne Type Classification Application**. This application uses a state-of-the-art hybrid deep learning model and YOLO inference backend to analyze skin images, classify acne severity, identify affected facial regions, and provide tailored clinical advice and preventive measures.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. User Registration & Authentication
To use the application, you must first register an account.

1. Navigate to the **User Register** page.
2. Fill out your details:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Password
   - Mobile Number
3. Click **Register**. 
4. **Note:** Newly registered accounts are **inactive** by default and require **Admin Approval** before you can sign in. An automated notification email is sent to the administrator to request approval.

---

## 🔑 Administrator Workflow

Administrators manage user access and can view logs of all acne predictions.

### 1. Admin Sign In
1. Navigate to the **Admin Login** page.
2. Sign in using your administrator credentials.

### 2. Approving New Users
1. Go to the **View Users** section from the navigation bar.
2. Under the **Pending Approvals** list, you will see a list of users who recently registered.
3. Click the **Approve** button next to a user's name to grant them access. 
4. Once approved, the user will receive an automated email notification confirming they can now log in.

### 3. Monitoring System History
- Admins can view all diagnostic records generated by users under the **Admin History** tab.

---

## 📸 User Workflow: Acne Diagnostics

Once approved, users can log in to access the diagnostic dashboard.

### 1. Navigating to the Prediction Page
Click **Upload & Predict** in the main menu to begin. You have two options for providing a skin image: **File Upload** or **Webcam Capture**.

---

### 2. Preparing Your Image (Do's & Don'ts Guide)
For accurate classification, ensure the photo meets the quality criteria shown on the page:

| ✅ Do's (Correct Image) | ❌ Don'ts (Incorrect Image) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Focus directly on the affected skin area. | Do not use heavily cropped passphoto layouts. |
| Use bright, natural, and even lighting. | Avoid blurry, dark, or out-of-focus shots. |
| Maintain a neutral facial expression close to the camera. | Avoid logos, watermarks, or text overlays. |

---

### 3. Option A: Uploading an Image File
1. Click the file selector area or drag and drop your image file.
2. Select the **Facial Region** (e.g., Forehead, Cheek, Nose, Chin, Jawline) where the acne is located.
3. Click **Get Prediction**.

---

### 4. Option B: Real-time Camera Capture (Desktop & Mobile)
1. Select the **Facial Region** where the acne is located.
2. Click the **Open Camera** card. 
3. The card will perform a smooth 3D flip animation, revealing the live camera viewport.
   - *On Desktop*: An interactive inline camera feed will open inside the card.
   - *On Mobile Devices*: The app will automatically launch your device's native front/back camera app to take a high-quality picture.
4. Line up your face/skin area in the camera viewport and click **Capture & Predict**.
5. To cancel and go back, click the **Back to File Browser** button at any time.

---

## 📊 Understanding the Analysis Results

Once the YOLO model processes the image, the results panel will display:

1. **Annotated Image Output**: The original image is displayed with bounding boxes highlighting detected acne spots.
2. **Acne Classification Severity**:
   - **Type**: (e.g., Papules, Pustules, Nodules, Blackheads)
   - **Severity Level**: Low, Moderate, or High.
3. **Region Diagnostics**:
   - **Why it occurs**: Possible root causes for acne in that specific facial region (e.g., dietary patterns, stress, cosmetics, hormone fluctuations).
   - **Prevention**: Practical changes to avoid future outbreaks.
   - **Home Remedies & Diet**: Recommended dietary updates and safe home care routines.

## 🗃️ Managing Your History

You can track your skin health improvements over time using the **My History** section.

### 1. WhatsApp-Style Selection
1. Hover over any card in your history list—a WhatsApp-style circular checkbox will fade in at the top-left corner of the card.
2. Click the card to select it. The card will display a vibrant active border glow and a green checkmark indicating selection.
3. To select all items on the page at once, check the **Select All** checkbox in the top action bar.

### 2. Bulk Deletion
1. Select one or more cards you wish to remove.
2. A status bar will appear showing the number of items selected (e.g., `3 items selected`).
3. Click the red trash icon (**Delete Selected**) to permanently remove those records.

### 3. Pagination
- Your history is paginated (10 records per page) to keep the dashboard performing fast and looking tidy. Use the page numbers at the bottom to browse older records.
